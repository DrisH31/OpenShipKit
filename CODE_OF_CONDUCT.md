# Code of Conduct

OpenShipKit follows the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.
The full text is at that link; this is a summary, not a replacement.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## In short

**Expected:** empathy and kindness; respect for differing opinions and
experiences; giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback; taking
responsibility for mistakes and learning from them; focusing on what is best
for the community.

**Not acceptable:** sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual
attention; trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or
political attacks; public or private harassment; publishing others’ private
information without their explicit permission; other conduct that could
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews — and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@DrisH31](https://github.com/DrisH31), through the contact page of the
website linked from that GitHub profile, starting the message with
“OpenShipKit Code of Conduct”. If the report is about the maintainer, or you’d
rather not write to them, use GitHub’s
[report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
form on the comment, issue or profile. (Security vulnerabilities go through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.) All reports are reviewed promptly and
fairly, and the reporter’s privacy is respected.

Maintainers follow the Covenant’s enforcement guidelines: **correction** (a
private warning), **warning** (with consequences for continued behaviour),
**temporary ban**, and **permanent ban**, depending on the impact.
